This is one member of eight: Earth to 2100 with emissions held at today's level and the deck of wild cards in play, each member drawing the deck with a different seed. Drag the bar to move through the years, or press play. Everything on this page follows the year the bar shows.

The run tells its own story as it goes. Each sentence is written by the part of the model that worked the thing out, in the year it happened. The newest is first.

Emissions enter a carbon account with sinks that weaken as the planet warms. The CO2 that stays in the air, with the other gases and aerosols, sets the forcing, and a two-layer ocean turns the forcing into warming. Up to 2025 the model can be compared with the measurements.

In 2024 the model is at +1.69 degrees against +1.54 measured, and 451 ppm against 425. Both parameter sets are recalled from the literature and nothing is tuned.

People are placed by country and, in the cell runs, by half-degree cell. The model counts those whose home climate has moved outside the range people have historically lived in, those past a wet-bulb threshold, and those in long heatwaves.

Tipping elements are counted as past their threshold when warming passes the published estimate, and some then release carbon. Wild cards, when the variant plays them, are drawn from a deck of speculative events by the run's seed.

Each country is scored on nine World Bank indicators and the scores move as heat and shocks arrive. From those pressures the model computes a world capacity to cut emissions, which in the derailed variants slows the planned cuts.

  • Every model parameter is a published value recalled from memory and labelled as such in each part's panel. Nothing is tuned.
  • The rules that move a country's score and the world's capacity are placeholders.
  • The wild-card deck and its chances are written from memory and unchecked.
  • The warming of each country scales with the global mean by a fitted slope that runs high for small islands and Gulf states.
  • The eight members differ only in their seed, so the spread between them is the deck's and nothing else's.
