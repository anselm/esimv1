This is the planet as one box, from 1850 to 2025: measured emissions go into a carbon account, and the CO2 left in the air warms a two-layer ocean. Drag the bar to move through the years.

The model takes each year's measured emissions and removes carbon to the land and ocean with sinks of fixed speeds. What is left is compared with the air measured at Mauna Loa and in the Law Dome ice.

The extra CO2 traps heat. A surface layer and a deep layer of ocean take it up, and the surface warming is compared with two measured records.

  • CO2 is the only forcing here, with no methane, aerosols or volcanoes. The model ends 2025 at 480 ppm against 425 measured.
  • The parameters are published values recalled from memory. Nothing is tuned.
  • This is the bottom rung of the planetary ladder, kept as a check. Earth 2100 is the full version.

This run was made before runs carried a description of their parts, so there are no panels to show here. The full data page lists every map and series it recorded.
