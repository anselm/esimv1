This is the simplest thing the engine can run: a tap pours into an upper bucket, which leaks into a lower one, which leaks away. It exists to check that the water account closes. Drag the bar to move through the month.

Each bucket fills until what leaks out equals what comes in. The two record at different rates on purpose, to test that parts on different clocks still agree.

This checks a formula and the bookkeeping. It says nothing about any real place.

This run was made before runs carried a description of their parts, so there are no panels to show here. The full data page lists every map and series it recorded.
