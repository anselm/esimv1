# 2026-11-01 — M10 Rules: a calendar row changes land use, water, money and deer

Overnight, same session (Claude, Fable 5.1, canada1). Seam test 5 of the brief, the hard
one, in this basin's terms.

## What was built

`rules.calendar`: reads a row from `data/events/calendar.toml` addressed to the rules
layer, logs it (`rule.loaded`, with its confidence, source and known-since date), and on
the row's effective date writes the constraint field it describes and publishes
`rule.effective`. The first rule is a land-use constraint: `rules.no_crop` is 1 within one
grid cell of any channel draining more than 20 km2. The farm agents obey it (those cells
become perennial grass), the crop module grows grass there (a standing canopy, never
harvested, with turnover to litter), and the water module gives those cells 30% more soil
capacity (a guess for deeper roots and better structure, named as a parameter).

The row itself is a what-if, marked speculative, with the note that Iowa's Nutrient
Reduction Strategy is voluntary and that a 450 m buffer is an order of magnitude wider
than practice, chosen so the effect is visible at 30 arc-seconds.

## The intervention, as a one-block manifest diff

`manifests/m10-rules-boone.toml` is `m9-farms-boone.toml` plus one module block. Year 2025:

| | without the rule | with the rule |
|---|---|---|
| buffer cells | | 1,275 of 3,500 (36% of the basin) |
| cropland | 209,900 ha | 129,559 ha |
| farm revenue | $126 M | $77 M |
| peak discharge at the gauge | 377 m3/s | 288 m3/s |
| mean discharge | 37.9 | 38.2 m3/s |
| basin GPP | 935 kt C | 760 kt C |
| deer at year end | 931 | 1,771 |
| ledgers | five closed | five closed |

The first attempt buffered channels above 2 km2 and covered 85% of this flat, drained
basin; the threshold was raised to 20 km2 and the fact recorded in the manifest. The
numbers are what the mechanism says, not what Iowa would do: the crop model is weak (M5,
M9) and the capacity factor is a guess. What matters is the chain: a dated row in a text
table reached the agents, the crop, the water and, unasked, the animals, which found
year-round grazing in the buffer and doubled. That last line is the kind of second-order
consequence the product exists to show, and it fell out of reduced outflows exactly as
Anselm's June 2026 test asked.

The integration test runs the season with and without the rule and asserts the buffer
removes cropland, moves the river's peak and lowers revenue, with every ledger closed.
