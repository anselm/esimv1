## What is new in it

The first scenario with no record. The Palisades and Fort McMurray were hindcasts: a fire that
happened, scored against perimeters, hotspots and inspections. This one is a year that has not
come, on a day nobody has seen, so three things had to be made up honestly.

- **Where.** The campsite is OpenStreetMap's (relation 17685328, 38.0316, -122.7328). The house
  is the US Census geocoder's interpolation of the street number along the TIGER line
  (38.0283, -122.6675), which puts it on the road centreline. No house number 550 exists in
  OpenStreetMap on that road, and no web search tied the address to Planetwork or Solstice Grove;
  the description is Anselm's. I did not look further at the parcel.
- **Wind.** `fire.cells` gained `wind_at`: the simulated instant that `wind_start` stands for. The
  HRRR hours are fetched for the record's days and indexed from `wind_at`; empty means
  `wind_start`, so every existing run is unchanged. The borrowed days are 27 to 29 October 2019,
  because the Bay Area forecast office's Red Flag Warning number 6 of 2019 (IEM VTEC archive,
  03Z 27 Oct to 18Z 28 Oct) covered the coastal North Bay and the North Bay mountains. At the
  campsite the 3 km analysis reads 1 to 8 m/s, north-east through the first afternoon, gusts to
  13, dead fuel moisture 3 to 5 percent. The ridges are not in a 3 km cell.
- **Stakes.** A module, `obs.places` (`crates/modules/src/places.rs`): named points with a note
  saying where each position came from. It publishes `place.marked` at the start so the views can
  stake and label them, reads `fire.burned_frac` each hour, reports the distance from each place to
  the nearest burned cell, and publishes `place.reached` and a `place.crossing` sentence once,
  when the place's own cell burns or a burned cell is within `reach_m` (50 m; the geocoded point
  is on the road, which carries no fuel, and the first run came within 25 m of it and never
  "arrived"). The 3D scene draws `place.marked` and `place.reached` as marks with labels that do
  not fade; the vertical stretch was already 2 (`ZS` in `Scene3D.svelte`), checked and kept.

## What the runs say

Four variants, each 9 s on the cached ground: thresholds times 3, 5 and 8, and 5 on the gust.

| variant | 12 h | 24 h | end | house reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| scale-3 | 21,995 acres | 35,256 | 46,749 (the window) | 19 h |
| scale-5 | 7,571 | 22,493 | 46,484 | 30 h (06:00 PDT, 2 Oct) |
| gust | 13,245 | 22,571 | 46,538 | 34 h |
| scale-8 | 0.6 | 0.6 | 1 | never |

Hours are from midnight PDT on 1 October; the ignition is at 3 a.m. The engine does not know:
between the Palisades setting and the Fort McMurray setting the fire either takes every cell in the
window in two days or never leaves its first cell. That is the honest headline and the README says
it. Two thirds of the window is WorldCover "tree" (172,822 cells), which the engine treats as one
fuel from a redwood canyon to a spruce stand, and nothing here scores it. The wind carries the fire
south-west first; the house, to the east, is reached when the ground between has burned.

## Not done, and next

- A ridge wind. A Marin fire day is the ridges; the 3 km analysis at the canyon floor is calm.
  Terrain-following wind has been the engine's next step since the Palisades.
- A fuel map that tells redwood from oak from grass and carries a load. WorldCover's "tree" is the
  weakest thing in this run.
- Scoring. There is nothing to score this against; the nearest thing would be the 2020 Woodward
  fire in Point Reyes, a few kilometres west on similar ground, with NIFC perimeters. Not fetched.
- An `onshore` variant on a hot September day with the sea breeze, so the fire runs east by wind
  rather than by filling. I did not find a sourced day in the time and did not guess one.
- The campsite is also listed as a place, so the run says the fire "reaches" it at the ignition
  hour. Harmless; a `role` on a place would tidy it.
- Not pushed and not deployed; the commits are local and say so.

## Later the same day: Rothermel

Anselm: "Let's add the rosenthal model also - a separate sim." Read as Rothermel, the first
Palisades engine; said so in the reply. `fire.rothermel` gained the same `wind_at` (midnight UTC of
`wind_start` stands for it; empty leaves every old run unchanged), and `manifests/f6-nicasio-2029-rothermel.toml`
is F5 with the engine swapped: the ERA5 point wind for 27 to 31 October 2019 from midnight UTC on
1 October 2029, the same ignition, the same places. Project `anselm/nicasio-2029-rothermel`, the
Palisades' four variants. Results: 15,884 acres by the end on the mean wind (6,053 at 24 h), 23,492
on the gusts; the fire stops 2,174 m from the house (1,325 m on the gusts) when the borrowed wind
drops on the second day; SH7 and the built barrier change nothing (160 shrub cells; the fire never
reaches the built land). Each run 4 s. The reanalysis point reads 3 to 8 m/s, gusts to 16, RH down
to 23%. The README puts the two engines side by side and says what that comparison is worth: an
agreement about the ground and the wind, a disagreement about the engines. Committed, unpushed.
