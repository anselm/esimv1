# 2026-11-03 — Bodies: every building its own thing to burn

The first iteration of the engine after the ground was fixed, and the first of the five steps in
`20261103-beyond-rothermel.md`. Anselm's ask (point 5 of his list in `todo.md`): "more clearly
indicate when actual buildings themselves are likely burned - right now they stay one color",
and behind it point 7, a per-plot assessment people could use.

## What was built

`fire.cells` gained `structures = "on"`. Every one of the 13,292 county footprints in the window
becomes a body: a centroid, a footprint area, the cell it stands in, the inspectors' word on its
fate (joined to the footprint by the county: 6,352 destroyed, 3,780 undamaged, the rest damaged
or uninspected), a fixed log-normal vulnerability factor, its neighbours within `house_reach_m`
measured as the gap between footprints rather than between centres (119,736 links at 40 m), and
the wildland cells within `exposure_m` whose fire it feels (88,722 cell-to-structure exposures).
Each substep a structure takes heat from burning cells near it and from burning neighbours
across the gap, both reaching further downwind, cools between doses, ignites past its threshold
times its factor, burns for `structure_burn_min` as a source for its neighbours, and lights the
ground it stands on so the cell engine carries the fire on. An ember landing in a cell with a
structure may take it (`ember_accept`, divided by the factor). Nine declared guesses.

Outputs: `building_fate` (the inspectors' word per cell, once) and `building_arrival_h` (each
building's own arrival hour, every six hours, the last frame holding them all) on the
one-arc-second grid; reports `fire.buildings_ignited`, `fire.buildings_burning`,
`fit.buildings_destroyed_reached`, `fit.buildings_undamaged_reached`, `fit.buildings_recall`,
`fit.buildings_precision`, scored per footprint rather than per cell. The 3D view colours every
box by the body's state against the record at the cursor: grey unburned and not destroyed, red
burned and destroyed, orange burned in the model but not destroyed in the record, blue destroyed
in the record and not reached by the model. That last colour is the one that matters.

## What the runs say

First guesses (threshold 3, gap scale 8 m, jitter 0.3) lit 12,837 of 13,171 bodies: a burning
house at a 10 m gap delivered seventeen times the threshold over its hour, so every neighbourhood
the cell fire touched burned to the last house. A sweep with `--set` on the project:

| threshold | gap scale | jitter | lit | destroyed reached | undamaged reached | recall | precision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 8 | 0.3 | 12,837 | 6,194 | 3,687 | 0.975 | 0.483 |
| 10 | 6 | 0.6 | 11,447 | 5,953 | 3,027 | 0.937 | 0.520 |
| 15 | 6 | 0.6 | 10,055 | 5,396 | 2,587 | 0.849 | 0.537 |
| 25 | 6 | 0.6 | 6,283 | 3,243 | 1,601 | 0.511 | 0.516 |

The base rate, destroyed among all bodies with a recorded fate, is 0.48. Precision never leaves
it. Raising the threshold trades recall for nothing: the model lights destroyed and undamaged
houses in the same proportion at every setting, which means the spatial pattern of who burned
carries almost no information the engine can see. The survivors stand between the destroyed, on
the same streets, in the same wind. Whatever made the difference is not in the footprint, the
height, the gap to the neighbour or the slope.

That is the finding, and it is the one the structure-ignition literature would have predicted:
the house's own vulnerability decides. We do not have it. The public inspection view carries two
fields, damage and structure type; the full form (roof, eaves, vents, windows, decks, year built,
defensible space) is in CAL FIRE's data-share view, which is not public. The county's footprint
service does carry `AIN`, the assessor's parcel number, and the assessor's roll (year built,
effective year, square footage, use) is public county data. That join is the next step for this
module, and it is the step that could move precision off the prior.

Two variants are kept: `bodies` (threshold 15: recall 0.85, precision 0.54) and `bodies-hard`
(threshold 25: 0.51 and 0.52), so the trade is visible on the compare page. The `--set` sweep
runs were pruned.
