## Why this project exists

Another attempt at a whole-system simulator: near-term futures at parcel, bioregion and planet
scale, many representations coupled with conservation at every seam, output as ensembles with
the mechanism visible. Anselm wants it to end as an appliance, "something similar to ESRI, but
with a stronger consumer facing facade", and expects that facade to be conversational. The
full brief he pasted is `20261031-design-brief.md` in this folder.

## First milestones

Proposed by the agent on day one, not yet agreed by Anselm:

1. Seam tests 1 and 2 from the brief (two-rate fields with an energy ledger; rain to reach
   network with a water ledger) on a new small kernel: conservative multi-rate scheduler,
   (time, priority, sequence) ordering, append-only event log, ledgers that close.
2. Pick one worked chain as the spine (the agent argues for Gulf hypoxia; it has a forty-year
   scoreboard and touches all five representations) and grow the seam tests inside it rather
   than as abstract exercises.

## Open questions

- Product first or coupler first. His message asks for a consumer facade; the brief rules UX
  out of scope. The agent's view: the brief is right, and the conversational facade is cheap
  once manifests and event logs exist (an LLM edits a manifest and narrates a log).
- Language and runtime. Every prior attempt was Node/ES6. The brief wants typed parallel
  arrays, determinism under parallelism, and maybe native numerics for a few fields. Default
  stays JavaScript until a seam test proves it cannot.
- Where the hydrology and DEM exploration lives (see Rests on).
- Does this supersede orbital-sim, or sit beside it as its engine?
