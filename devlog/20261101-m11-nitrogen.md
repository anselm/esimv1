# 2026-11-01 — M11 Nitrogen: fertilizer to gauge, six ledgers, a five-year hindcast scored

Overnight, same session (Claude, Fable 5.1, canada1). The hypoxia chain now runs from the
farm agents' fertilizer decision to nitrate concentration at the river gauge.

## What was built

`nitrogen.basin`: per cell, a mineral nitrogen pool fed by fertilizer on the agents'
planting day at the agents' rate and by mineralisation of a large organic pool (named for
what Iowa's mollisols do), drawn down by crop uptake in proportion to the crop module's
daily carbon gain (soybean fixing half from the air), by denitrification when wet, and by
leaching in proportion to the fraction of soil water that drained that day (a new field
`water.drain_daily_mm`). Harvest exports grain nitrogen; residue returns to the organic
pool. Leached nitrate is handed to the water module through a field and a signal; the
water module carries it as a tracer through the same linear-reservoir routing as the
water and reports load and concentration at the pour point beside the observed series,
which it now fetches too (USGS parameter 99133).

A sixth ledger, `nitrogen` in kg N, spans two modules: the nitrogen module declares the
soil and crop pools, the water module declares `channel_n`. The first hindcast leaked 7 g
against daily flows of 55 t, the same precision defect as M2 (an f64 sum transferred, f32
cells published). It is fixed the same way and the rule is now written in the README.
All six ledgers close over 1,826 daily closes, the worst at 2.6e-5 kg N.

## The hindcast and its score

`manifests/m11-nitrogen-boone.toml`: 2013 to 2017, the years with daily nitrate at the
gauge (1,017 days with a value in the window; USGS 05481000, mean 12.0 mg/L as N,
maximum 30.9). ERA5 on a 0.5-degree point grid (the 0.25-degree five-year request hit a
streaming timeout on Open-Meteo's side, which the fetcher had cached as if it were data;
it now refuses non-JSON and retries). 75 s, 485,745 events, 191,750 delivered.

Read honestly: the seasonal shape is right (a spring peak after fertilizer and the first
drainage, a late-summer trough, an autumn rise), and the magnitude is about twice the
river in concentration and three times in load. Three known causes, in order: the crop
takes up a third of what a real crop would (M5's weak growth, now visible as 30 kg N/ha
of uptake against 85 applied), so most of the nitrogen has nowhere to go but the drains;
the water module's wet bias (+77% over these years) multiplies the load; and the
leaching rule sends the drained fraction of all mineral nitrogen at once, with no
retention. Published Corn Belt losses to tile drains are of the order of 25 to 35 kg
N/ha/yr (the agent's recollection), against 122 here. None of this is calibrated; the
hindcast harness is what calibrates it, and the parameters that matter are named.

## What this rung proves

The brief's spine chain exists end to end: weather and sun drive a soil, the soil feeds a
crop, agents decide the fertilizer, the nitrogen follows the water through a network
derived from real terrain to a real gauge with a real record, and six conserved
quantities close across all of it every day for five years. The hypoxia end (SPARROW-style
delivery to the Gulf and the July cruise) is the next link and is a routing problem over
a bigger network, not a new mechanism.
