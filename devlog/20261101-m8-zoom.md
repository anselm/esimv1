# 2026-11-01 — M8 Zoom: a cohort becomes individuals and back, count conserved

Overnight, same session (Claude, Fable 5.1, canada1). The research rung of the ladder,
done for one population and one box; the nested-grid half is not done and is said so.

## What was built

Inside a box (42.6 to 42.8 N, 93.95 to 93.75 W, about 22 by 16 km in the middle of the
Boone River basin) and between two dates (1 June to 1 September 2025), the deer cohort of
M6 is represented as individuals with positions. Outside the box, and before and after,
it stays a density per cell. The switch is exact by construction:

- Zoom in: each cell's count splits into a whole number of individuals, placed at random
  positions in the cell, and a fractional residue that stays as cohort mass. Nothing is
  rounded away.
- While zoomed: individuals eat from the same per-cell intake as the cohort, are born
  and die by Bernoulli draws at the same daily rates the cohort uses continuously, are
  killed by the predator in whole numbers with the fractional part of each day's demand
  carried to the next day, and walk 400 m a day, half toward the richest neighbouring
  cell and half noise. An individual that steps outside the box becomes one unit of
  cohort count in the cell it stepped into. One that would step outside the basin stays.
- Zoom out: every individual becomes one unit of count in its cell; residues merge back.
- The declared stock on the population ledger is counts plus residues plus individuals,
  whatever the representation; the ledger must close across all of it.

## What happened

`manifests/m8-zoom-boone.toml`, year 2025, 17 s. On 1 June, 654 animals in the box became
individuals. Over 89 days, 473 of them crossed the box edge and folded back into counts;
228 remained as individuals on 31 August and folded back on 1 September. The two switch
errors were -6e-12 and -1e-11 animals (floating point on sums of thousands). The
population ledger closed every day to 3e-10 animals across the switches, the walks, the
boundary crossings, predation in whole numbers, and Bernoulli births and deaths. Carbon,
energy and water closed beside it. The integration test runs a spring version and also
resumes the run from a snapshot taken while zoomed and replays it exactly, so the
individual state, the residues and the random stream all round-trip.

A first attempt only converted the animals and let them be eaten: an edit meant to add
the individual-level dynamics matched nothing in the file and did nothing, which the
"no individual ever left the box" number exposed. The lesson for the tooling: a silent
no-op edit is a bug; the agent now checks that an edit matched before building on it.

## What this proves, and what it does not

Proved: the brief's claim that a population can switch representation with its count
conserved, with the ledger as the witness, and that the switch composes with everything
else in the stack (grazing, predation, fire flight, replay). The same mechanism will let
households be a density at planet scale and agents in a city.

Not done: nested grids, where a coarse run supplies boundary conditions to a fine run of
the same modules inside it. That is a grid-to-grid translator for fields (conserving
fluxes across the boundary), not a population switch, and it remains the open mechanism.
Also not done: the viewer cannot draw points yet, so the individuals are visible only as
the density field they contribute to.
