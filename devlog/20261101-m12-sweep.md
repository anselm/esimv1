# 2026-11-01 — M12 begins: calibration by method, and a structural ceiling

Overnight, same session (Claude, Fable 5.1, canada1). The last thing built before dawn.

## What was built

`esim sweep <manifest> --score <sim> <obs> --samples N --set module.param=lo:hi ...`:
Latin-hypercube samples of the named parameters (seeded, so the design is reproducible),
one deterministic run per sample across threads, each scored at matching times, the table
written to `runs/<id>/sweep.json` with the best ten printed. It is the first form of the
hindcast harness's calibration: nothing is chosen by hand, the table is the record, and
the manifest line that adopts a parameter can cite the sweep that found it.

## The result

`manifests/m3-water-boone-30s.toml` (the M3 stack at 30 arc-seconds, station rain), 64
samples over soil capacity (60 to 320 mm), drainage (0.004 to 0.10 per day), evaporation
factor (0.4 to 1.3), channel velocity (0.2 to 1.2 m/s) and degree-day factor (1 to 6),
scored on 2025 daily discharge at USGS 05481000. 84 s on 8 threads.

The defaults chosen on the first evening (150 mm, 0.03, 0.7, 0.4, 3) scored 0.469 at 15
arc-seconds. The best of 64 samples lands in the same place, with the bias traded against
the peaks exactly as the two hand variants on the first night suggested. Sixty-four is a
small design for five dimensions, so this is not proof of a global ceiling; it is strong
evidence that the parameters are not where the remaining skill is. The structure is: no
frozen ground in winter, no tile-drain dynamics beyond one linear term, no separate fast
and slow pathways, one rain gauge for 2,000 km2. That is the list for the next version of
the water module, and the sweep is how its improvement will be shown rather than claimed.

## What M12 still needs

- Multi-year objective: the nitrate hindcast (2013 to 2017) and the discharge hindcast
  should be scored together, with the knowledge cutoff from the event tables applied.
- Multi-objective and multi-site scoring; a proper sampler (the sweep is one round of a
  Latin hypercube, not an optimiser) and a posterior rather than a single best row.
- The forecast verification command for ensembles (coverage of the band, CRPS), owed
  since F1.
