# 2026-11-01 — M9 Takers: farm agents write the fields the crop reads, money closes

Overnight, same session (Claude, Fable 5.1, canada1). The first human rung.

## What runs

`agents.farms`: the basin's cells grouped into 243 blocks of 4 by 4 cells (about 13 km2
each, a neighbourhood of Iowa farms, not a farm). Each block is an agent with cash, a
rotation memory and three rules. On day 105 it chooses corn or soybean by expected margin
(expected yield times price minus input cost, with a rotation bonus for corn after
soybean and a penalty for corn on corn). From then, once five consecutive days average
10 C and the block's soil is below 85% of capacity, it plants, paying its costs. It writes
three fields the crop module now reads instead of its own rule: `agents.crop_kind`,
`agents.plant_now` and `agents.n_rate_kg_ha` (the last for M11). At harvest the crop
module writes `veg.grain_export_kgc` and publishes `harvest.done`; the agent converts
carbon to bushels and sells at the season's price. The crop module gained soybean as a
second parameter set and a perennial grass cover for cells a rule forbids to crop (M10).

A `money` ledger in dollars closes daily across `market`, `farms` (declared stock: the
blocks' cash) and `suppliers_and_land`. Five ledgers now close together: animals, carbon,
energy, money, water. Year 2025, 17 s.

## Numbers, and why they are wrong

| | |
|---|---|
| corn / soybean | 102,892 / 107,007 ha (a rotation, once corn-on-corn carried a penalty; without it every block chose corn) |
| planting | all 243 blocks on 20 April, the first day the rules allowed |
| harvest | 5 October |
| corn yield | 65 bu/acre |
| soybean yield | 21 bu/acre |
| revenue | $126 M |
| cash at year end | -$218 M, about -$1,036 per hectare |

Iowa corn yields about 200 bu/acre and soybean about 60 (the agent's recollection, not
fetched). The farms lose money because the crop module grows a third of a real crop (M5
already said so: biomass peaks at half of real and the harvest index is a guess), while
the costs are gross figures of the agent's recollection. The scoreboard for this rung is
USDA county yields and prices; the NASS Quick Stats API needs a key, so nothing was
fetched and nothing here is scored. The rung proves the seam, not the economics: agents
decide, write fields, a population obeys them, the harvest comes back as money, and the
intervention mechanism is ready, because a rule module can now forbid cropping in a cell
and the agents and the crop will follow.
