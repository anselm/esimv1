# Sources

Every record this simulator reads, from `data/catalog.toml`, with the publisher, the licence as the catalog records it, and the day it was last verified from this machine. Generated by `esim sources --write`; edit the catalog, not this page. Several licence texts are still to be fetched and stored; those entries say so.

## assessment series

### IPCC AR6 WG1 effective radiative forcing time series, 1750 to 2019, by component

- **id** `ipcc-ar6-erf`
- **publisher** IPCC AR6 WG1 Chapter 7 (Forster et al. 2021), time series produced by the chapter's authors; GitHub chrisroadmap/ar6; citable copy Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.5705391
- **licence** Zenodo record 5705391 (AR6 WG1 Annex III Extended Data, Smith et al.) states CC BY 4.0, read from the record API on 2026-11-01; the GitHub repository's own licence file to be read and stored before promotion
- **units** W/m2, effective radiative forcing relative to 1750
- **verified** 2026-11-01 from canada1: 270 rows 1750 to 2019; aerosol 2019 = see the run's forcing.ar6_aerosol_w_m2 series
- **url** `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chrisroadmap/ar6/main/data_output/AR6_ERF_1750-2019.csv`
- **notes** An assessment, not an observation: these are the IPCC's best-estimate forcings. Used for the components the model does not compute (everything but CO2, CH4, N2O), with the stated assumption after 2019.

- **id** `open-meteo-gefs`
- **publisher** Open-Meteo, serving NOAA Global Ensemble Forecast System (GEFS)
- **licence** Open-Meteo API: free for non-commercial use only. GEFS itself is US federal public domain and is also published directly by NOAA.
- **units** as open-meteo-era5
- **verified** 2026-11-01 from canada1: 31 members for the Boone window, past 10 days and 10 days ahead, used by the F1 forecast
- **url** `https://ensemble-api.open-meteo.com/v1/ensemble?latitude={lat}&longitude={lon}&hourly=temperature_2m,precipitation,shortwave_radiation,relative_humidity_2m,wind_speed_10m&models=gfs_seamless&past_days={past_days}&forecast_days={forecast_days}`
- **notes** The exact model name and the member count in the query are as the fetcher sends them; see crates/registry/src/obs.rs.

### NOAA Global Historical Climatology Network Daily, by station

- **id** `ghcn-daily`
- **publisher** NOAA NCEI, distributed on the AWS Open Data registry (bucket noaa-ghcn-pds)
- **licence** US federal public domain; WMO Resolution 40 applies to some non-US stations
- **units** precipitation and temperature in tenths of mm and tenths of C in the file; the reader converts to mm and C
- **verified** 2026-10-31 from canada1: station USC00138806 (Webster City, IA, 42.4686 N 93.7975 W) has PRCP, SNOW, TMAX, TMIN 1893 to 2026; 2025 has 362 precipitation days totalling 1,089.8 mm, observation time 0700 local
- **url** `https://noaa-ghcn-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/csv/by_station/{station}.csv`
- **notes** Rows with a quality flag are dropped by the reader. Daily totals are for the 24 hours ending at the observation time.

### Open-Meteo historical weather API (ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis), hourly by point

- **id** `open-meteo-era5`
- **publisher** Open-Meteo, serving ECMWF ERA5 and ERA5-Land reanalysis (Copernicus Climate Change Service)
- **licence** Open-Meteo API: free for non-commercial use only; a commercial tier exists. The underlying ERA5 data are under the Copernicus licence. Exact texts to be fetched and stored before promotion.
- **units** temperature_2m degC, precipitation mm, shortwave_radiation W/m2, relative_humidity_2m %, wind_speed_10m m/s
- **verified** 2026-10-31 and 2026-11-01 from canada1: anonymous requests for the Boone window at 0.25 and 0.5 degree point spacing; the archive lags about five days; the API sometimes answers a non-JSON streaming timeout, which the fetcher refuses and retries
- **url** `https://archive-api.open-meteo.com/v1/archive?latitude={lat}&longitude={lon}&start_date={start}&end_date={end}&hourly=temperature_2m,precipitation,shortwave_radiation,relative_humidity_2m,wind_speed_10m`
- **notes** A paid service must not rely on this endpoint; pull ERA5 from the Climate Data Store or another licensed source into the shared cache instead.

## reanalysis series

### NASA POWER daily regional: MERRA-2 2 m temperature maximum and dew point per day on the 0.5 x 0.625 degree grid, by 10-degree box and year

- **id** `nasa-power-daily-regional`
- **publisher** NASA Langley Research Center POWER project (Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources), funded through the NASA Earth Science Division; meteorology from MERRA-2 (GMAO). POWER Daily API v2.10.1 on 2026-11-02
- **licence** US federal (NASA) data; POWER asks that its reference be included and does not state a restriction (docs/referencing, read 2026-11-02); no licence text found on the site
- **units** degC; -999 is missing; days in local solar time
- **verified** 2026-11-02 from canada1: a 10x10 box over India for 2010, 357 points, 3.5 MB CSV or 2.2 MB JSON in about a second; the API refuses more than 10 degrees a side or more than 366 days; six requests back to back answered in 0.4 s each
- **url** `https://power.larc.nasa.gov/api/temporal/daily/regional?parameters={param}&community=RE&longitude-min={lon0}&longitude-max={lon1}&latitude-min={lat0}&latitude-max={lat1}&start={year}0101&end={year}1231&format=JSON`
- **notes** T2M_MAX is the maximum hourly 2 m temperature of the day, T2MDEW the day's dew point, both MERRA-2 at about 50 km. The cells wet-bulb layer uses them as the station layer used GSOD: an afternoon wet-bulb per day (Stull) shifted at constant humidity. A reanalysis, not a measurement; the station layer stays as its check.

## Thresholds and tables

`data/events/tipping.toml` carries the climate tipping elements with a source and a confidence per row (every row recalled from Armstrong McKay et al. 2022 until checked); `data/events/calendar.toml` and `hazards.toml` carry dated rules and hazards with their sources. The human climate niche (Xu et al. 2020), the wet-bulb limits (Vecellio et al. 2022; Sherwood and Huber 2010), the carbon response (Joos et al. 2013; Millar et al. 2017), the energy balance (Held et al. 2010; Geoffroy et al. 2013), the gas forcings (Myhre et al. 1998) and the wet-bulb formula (Stull 2011) are cited in each kind's describe, marked recalled where not yet checked against the paper.
